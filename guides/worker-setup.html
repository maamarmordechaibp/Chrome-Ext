Catalog Generator — Setup Guide

Follow these steps once to install the extension, then sign in and start building catalogs.

Before you start: your administrator must first create your account. You'll receive an email address and a temporary password — you'll need them in Step 6.

Part A — Install the extension (once)

  1. Download the extension. Open this link and save the file:
    https://catalog-ext.pages.dev/dist.zip
  2. Unzip it. Right-click the downloaded dist.zip → Extract All… → pick an easy, permanent folder such as Documents\CatalogExtension. Keep this folder — don't delete it. The extension runs from here.
  3. Open Chrome's extensions page. Type this in the address bar and press Enter:
    chrome://extensions
  4. Turn on “Developer mode” — the switch in the top-right corner of that page.
  5. Click “Load unpacked” (top-left), then select the folder you unzipped in Step 2. The Catalog Generator card appears. Click the puzzle-piece 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar and pin it.

Part B — Sign in

  1. Click the extension icon in the toolbar to open it.
  2. Enter the email & password your administrator gave you, then click Sign In.

  3. Tip: click the ⧉ button (top-right) to pop the tool into its own window that stays open while you browse.

Part C — Build a catalog

  1. Go to a marketplace search page in Chrome — Amazon, Walmart, eBay, or AliExpress — and search for your product.
  2. Open the extension → Generate tab. It should show a green ✓ Amazon (or the site you're on) and the number of products found. If it says “Not a supported page,” click ↻ Refresh.
  3. Fill Customer & Representative (optional), pick how many pages to scan, then click 🔍 Scan Products. The page will scroll itself to load every image — that's normal.
  4. Click 📄 Generate PDF. When it's done you can ⬇️ Download, 📠 Fax, or ✉️ Email it.

Part D — When a customer calls back

  1. Open an item: go to the Open Item tab, type the item number the customer reads you (e.g. 1047), and click 🔍 Open Product Page. The exact product opens in a new tab.
    You only need the item number — not the long Catalog ID.
  2. Resend a whole catalog: on the same tab, under Resend a catalog, paste the Catalog ID (looks like CAT-20260706-000123) and click 📠 Fax or ✉️ Email.

Getting updates

When a new version is released, re-download https://catalog-ext.pages.dev/dist.zip, unzip it over the same folder (replace files), then on chrome://extensions click the ↻ reload icon on the Catalog Generator card.

Troubleshooting

  1. “Not a supported page.” You're not on an Amazon/Walmart/eBay/AliExpress search page, or the page hasn't finished loading. Refresh the web page, then click ↻ Refresh in the tool.
  2. “Incorrect email or password.” Ask your administrator to reset your password on the admin site.
  3. “No account? Ask your administrator.” Your account hasn't been created yet — contact your admin.
  4. An item says “no saved product link.” That catalog was made before the latest update. Re-generate it.
